CardHaulPrivacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

CardHaul is a desktop app for macOS and Windows that uploads files from cameras and memory cards to the user's own Google Drive. This policy explains what data CardHaul accesses and how it's handled.

Google user data CardHaul accesses

  • Google Drive files created by CardHaul, through the drive.file scope. This scope only allows access to files and folders CardHaul itself creates (the "CardHaul" folder and the files uploaded into it). CardHaul cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive.
  • Basic account details available through the Drive API: your email address and your Drive storage quota, so CardHaul can show which account is connected and how much space is free.

How the data is used

CardHaul uses this access only to upload files from devices you choose, to verify the uploaded copies (by comparing sizes and checksums), and to show your account and free space in its settings. It uses your data for no other purpose.

Storage and sharing

  • All processing happens on your own computer. CardHaul has no servers; your files go directly from your computer to Google.
  • Your Google sign-in token is stored in your operating system's secure storage: the macOS Keychain or the Windows Credential Manager.
  • CardHaul's settings and upload history are stored only on your computer.
  • CardHaul contains no advertising, analytics or tracking, and sends no data to the developer or any third party.
  • Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

CardHaul's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Choose Sign out in CardHaul's settings to revoke its access and delete the stored token, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling CardHaul removes the app; files already uploaded stay in your Google Drive until you delete them.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
