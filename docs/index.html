CardHaul

Plug in your camera. Footage goes to Google Drive. You're told when it's safe to wipe the card.

CardHaul is a free, open-source app for macOS and Windows. When you plug in a camera, drone or SD card, it uploads the new files straight to a CardHaul folder in your Google Drive, checks every file against the uploaded copy, and notifies you when it's safe to clear the device.

What it does

  • One-way: files are only added to Drive. Nothing in Drive is ever deleted or overwritten, so clearing your card never touches your uploads.
  • Verified: every file is compared with Google's copy by checksum (or by size, if you prefer speed) before you're told it's safe.
  • No local copies: files stream from the device to Drive, so it works on computers with little free space.
  • Automatic: runs from the menu bar or system tray; uploads start when a device you've added is plugged in. Pause or cancel at any time.

Your Google account

CardHaul uses Google sign-in with the narrowest Drive permission available: it can only see and manage files it created itself. It can't read anything else in your Drive. See the privacy policy.
